About Keerikattu Jewellery

Rooted in the heart of Thodupuzha for decades, our brand has been a trusted custodian of Kerala's finest jewelry traditions. Located on Temple Road, we blend time-honored heritage with modern luxury, specializing in premium sterling silver and high-finish 1-gram gold covering. Every piece we craft, from sacred traditional designs to modern everyday essentials, reflects our unwavering commitment to exquisite artistry and brilliant perfection.
